## 人物立绘 最终版

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE game-ready sprite atlas of SIX ORIGINAL Chinese characters in the very simple low-resolution portrait visual language of the supplied Papers, Please screenshot, which is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not reproduce the reference character. Exactly 3 columns x 2 rows, six equal square cells, no borders or gutters. Flat solid identical background #202720 in every cell. Each character FRONT FACING cropped at chest, large head, centered with margin, no hands. Crucial: each cell MUST look as if drawn at ONLY 64 x 64 native pixels then enlarged 8x using nearest neighbor. VERY LARGE SQUARE PIXEL BLOCKS, stair-step silhouette edges. Use only 5 to 7 FLAT colors per person: shadow, midtone, single highlight skin tone, 2 clothing colors, 1 hair color. Eyes only tiny horizontal dark bars, nose only 3-4 pixel stepped shape, mouth a single dark line. Flat poster-like shadow shapes, uneven human faces, stern quiet expressions. No dithering, no small texture, no detail rendering, no gradients, no smooth contours, no lighting effects, no painterly marks, no photorealism, no anime. Face should have the crude geometric simplicity of the bald man on the LEFT of reference. Top left woman age 35 short dark bob round glasses, olive jacket; top middle man age 55 broad square face gray short hair blue work jacket; top right older woman age 65 gray short curly hair purple cardigan; bottom left man age 30 side parted hair cream shirt and dark vest; bottom middle woman age 28 chin length black hair tan jacket; bottom right young male visitor age 20 tousled black hair blue shirt. Background is perfectly flat and empty. No text, labels, frames, props, captions. Three by two equal cells. Output landscape 3:2.
